What Is a Discount Authority Matrix?

A Discount Authority Matrix defines who can approve discounts based on deal size, discount depth, and customer segment. This tool helps you design clear approval workflows, establish governance, and generate policy documentation for consistent pricing execution.

Key Features

Visual Matrix Builder

Interactive matrix design with color-coded authority levels for easy visualization.

Role Hierarchy

Define approval roles from sales rep to executive with clear escalation paths.

Multi-Dimension Tiers

Configure deal size tiers, discount ranges, and customer segments.

Escalation Rules

Visual escalation flow showing approval chain for any scenario.

Conditional Rules

Add special rules for new customers, competitive situations, or strategic accounts.

Policy Generation

Auto-generate professional policy documents ready for distribution.
