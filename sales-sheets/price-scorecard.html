What Is a Price Execution Scorecard?

A Price Execution Scorecard provides a comprehensive, multi-dimensional assessment of how well your organization executes pricing strategies. By measuring performance across key dimensions, you can identify strengths, weaknesses, and prioritize improvements that drive profitability.

Key Features

Multi-Dimensional Scoring

Evaluate execution across 7 key dimensions with weighted scoring for overall performance.

Entity Benchmarking

Compare teams, regions, and products to identify best practices and gaps.

Radar Visualization

Visual representation of performance across all scoring dimensions.

Performance Rankings

Rank entities from top performers to those needing attention.

Improvement Priorities

Automatically identify high-impact improvement opportunities.

Trend Analysis

Track progress over time and measure improvement efforts.

Scoring Dimensions

Price Compliance (20%) - Adherence to approved prices
Discount Accuracy (15%) - Correct discount application
Response Speed (15%) - Quote turnaround time
Win Rate (15%) - Deal conversion success
Margin Achievement (20%) - Target margin attainment
List Price Adherence (10%) - Starting price discipline
Approval Compliance (5%) - Following approval processes
