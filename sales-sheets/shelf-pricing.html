Shelf Price Optimizer

Optimize Prices Based on Shelf Position, Facings, and Competitor Proximity

The Challenge

Products at different shelf positions have varying visibility and consideration rates. Eye-level products enjoy 25-35% higher visibility, while bottom-shelf items require price incentives to compete. Traditional pricing ignores these physical realities, leaving significant profit on the table.

Profit Opportunity

Key Features

  • Position-adjusted price elasticity modeling
  • Visibility scoring based on shelf level and facings
  • Competitor adjacency impact analysis
  • Interactive shelf layout visualization
  • Multi-objective optimization (profit/revenue/share)
  • Constrained price change recommendations
  • Export-ready optimization reports

Input Requirements

  • Product names and current prices
  • Product costs for margin analysis
  • Shelf position (eye/reach/stoop level)
  • Number of facings per product
  • Competitor adjacency indicators
  • Base price elasticity estimates

Business Applications

Category Management

Optimize shelf pricing across entire categories while respecting position-based visibility differences and competitive dynamics.

Planogram Optimization

Inform planogram decisions by understanding the profit implications of product placement and facing allocation.

Trade Negotiation

Quantify the value of premium shelf positions for trade negotiations with retailers and distributors.

Private Label Strategy

Price private label products optimally based on their typical shelf positions relative to national brands.

Analytical Output

  • Optimal price by shelf position
  • Position-adjusted elasticity values
  • Visibility scores by product
  • Profit impact projections
  • Volume change forecasts
  • Margin improvement estimates
  • Interactive shelf visualization
  • Downloadable results (CSV)
