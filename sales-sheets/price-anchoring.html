The Challenge

Customers don't evaluate prices in isolation. The first price they see (the anchor) dramatically influences their perception of subsequent prices. Without strategic anchoring, you're leaving significant perceived value on the table.

The Solution

The Price Anchoring Simulator lets you test different anchoring strategies, visualize their psychological impact, and optimize your price presentation for maximum perceived value.

Key Benefits

  • Visualize anchor-target price relationships
  • Model customer adjustment from anchor
  • Compare multiple pricing scenarios
  • Test high-anchor, charm pricing, and decoy strategies
  • Category-specific adjustments
  • Export analysis results

Supported Anchoring Strategies

High Anchor Strategy

Show a higher "original" price to make the target price seem like a compelling deal. Creates strong perceived savings.

Charm Pricing

Use $X.99 endings to appear significantly lower than round numbers. Small changes with measurable impact.

Decoy Effect

Add a less attractive option that makes your target option more appealing through comparison contrast.

Price Bracketing

Offer good-better-best options to guide customers toward the middle tier using compromise effect psychology.
