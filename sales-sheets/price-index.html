📈 Four Index Types

Laspeyres

Uses base period quantities as weights. Tends to overstate inflation.

Paasche

Uses current period quantities. Tends to understate inflation.

Fisher

Geometric mean of both. Most accurate overall measure.

CPI-Style

Weighted average approach used by government statistics.
