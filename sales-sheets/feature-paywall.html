The Challenge

Product teams struggle to decide which features belong in free vs. paid tiers. Too many valuable features in free reduces upgrade motivation. Too few creates friction and kills viral growth.

The Solution

Our Feature Paywall Optimizer uses a multi-dimensional scoring system to evaluate each feature across value, cost, stickiness, and viral potential - then recommends optimal tier placement.

Key Benefits

  • Data-driven tier placement decisions
  • Maximize viral growth potential
  • Optimize conversion triggers
  • Balance cost vs. value trade-offs
  • Visualize feature positioning
  • Project conversion impact

Scoring Dimensions

Dimension What It Measures Tier Impact
Value Score Perceived worth to users High value = Paid tier candidate
Cost Score Infrastructure & support costs Low cost = Free tier friendly
Stickiness Score User retention impact High stickiness = Paid tier lock-in
Viral Score Organic growth potential High viral = Free tier growth driver
