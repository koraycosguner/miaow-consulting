Pocket Price Dashboard

Monitor Price Realization and Track Revenue Leakage Across Your Organization

What Is Pocket Price?

Pocket price represents the actual revenue retained after all discounts, rebates, allowances, and deductions are applied. This dashboard provides complete visibility into your price waterfall, helping you identify and address revenue leakage at every stage of the pricing process.

Key Features

Price Waterfall Analysis

Visualize the complete path from list price to pocket price with detailed breakdowns of each deduction category.

Leakage Source Tracking

Identify and quantify every source of revenue leakage including discounts, rebates, and allowances.

Segment Comparisons

Compare pocket price realization across customer segments, products, and time periods.

Trend Analysis

Monitor pocket price trends over time to identify patterns and measure improvement efforts.

Invoice-Level Detail

Drill down to individual transactions to understand specific leakage patterns.

Export Capabilities

Export charts and data for presentations and further analysis.
